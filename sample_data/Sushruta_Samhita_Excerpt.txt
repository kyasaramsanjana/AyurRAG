CHAPTER 5: AGROPAHARANIYA - SURGICAL APPLIANCES AND PROTOCOLS
The surgeon should arrange all necessary implements before embarking on incision or cautery. The patient must be fortified with nutritious liquids.

CHAPTER 6: RITU CHARYA - SEASONAL EFFECT ON TISSUES
Seasonal transitions cause subtle changes in blood viscidity. In Sharad (Autumn), Pitta is naturally aggravated; cooling therapies and bitter herbs such as Neem and Chandana should be prescribed.

CHAPTER 8: SHASTRAS - PROPERTIES OF BLUNT AND SHARP INSTRUMENTS
Cleanliness and sterilization with boiling decoctions prevents sepsis. Honey and ghee smeared over wounds prevent excessive trauma and promote epithelial regeneration.
