CHAPTER 1: SUTRA STHANA - LONGEVITY AND NUTRITION
Ayurveda is the science of life. To protect health and cure disease, one must observe the equilibrium of Dhatus and Doshas.

CHAPTER 2: APAMARGA TANDULIYA - DETOXIFICATION
Herbs for internal purification eliminate morbid humors through upper and lower tracts. Pippali (long pepper) stimulates digestion, expels cold phlegm, and enhances metabolic absorption.

CHAPTER 3: ARAGVADHIYA - MEDICATED LEPIDAS AND PASTES
Application of Aragvadha (Cassia fistula) and Haridra alleviates skin diseases, itching, and systemic toxic accumulations.
